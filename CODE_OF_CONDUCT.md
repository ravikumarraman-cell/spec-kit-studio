# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want a welcoming, professional community for people of every background and experience level. Participate with respect, assume good intent, and focus discussion on ideas, evidence, and the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific in feedback.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Protect private information, credentials, and confidential repository material.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately misleading maintainers or contributors about safety, security, test results, or provenance.

## Reporting and enforcement

Until maintainers configure a private reporting channel, use the repository's maintainer contact shown in its published profile rather than a public issue for conduct concerns. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect confidentiality where possible, and may warn, remove content, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant depending on severity and pattern.

This document is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant, with project-specific reporting guidance.
